01 / WHO WE ARE

Investigate first.
Conclude carefully.

Essex Paranormal is an independent paranormal investigation team built around honest reporting, real experiences and unbiased evidence review.

We approach every investigation with an open mind while remaining prepared to question what we see, hear and record. The aim is not to prove that everything is paranormal. The aim is to investigate properly and document what can and cannot be explained.
