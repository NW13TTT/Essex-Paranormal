THE PERSON BEHIND
THE INVESTIGATION.

Essex Paranormal is built around a simple principle: investigate first, conclude second.

Lewis approaches paranormal investigation with curiosity, respect for locations and a commitment to examining evidence honestly. Every experience is treated as an opportunity to ask better questions rather than force a predetermined answer.

From historic buildings and reported haunted locations to personal experiences, the aim is to document what happens and separate observation from interpretation.
