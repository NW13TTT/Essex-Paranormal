WHO WE ARE

Paranormal investigation without the theatre.

Essex Paranormal exists to investigate reported paranormal activity honestly, respectfully and without sensationalism.

We document what happens during an investigation and analyse the evidence afterwards.

We do not begin an investigation assuming that a location is haunted, and we do not manufacture evidence to create a better story.

Sometimes we find something unusual. Sometimes we find a perfectly natural explanation.

Both outcomes matter.
