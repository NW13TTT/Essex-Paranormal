MEET LEWIS

THE PERSON BEHIND THE INVESTIGATION.

Essex Paranormal was created around a simple principle: investigate properly, document what happens and allow the evidence to speak for itself.

Lewis has carried out a number of paranormal investigations and continues to approach each location with curiosity rather than predetermined conclusions.

The aim is not to prove that every unexplained experience is paranormal. The aim is to understand what happened, document it accurately and separate genuine unexplained evidence from ordinary explanations wherever possible.

Every investigation is different. Every location presents its own challenges. The process remains the same: observe, question, record and review.

01

HONEST

Findings are presented honestly, including evidence that does not support a paranormal explanation.

02

REAL

Real people, real locations and real investigations documented as they happen.

03

UNBIASED

Evidence is reviewed without forcing an explanation onto an unexplained event.

WHAT WE DO

EXPERIENCES WORTH INVESTIGATING.

Paranormal investigation is about more than equipment. It is about people, places, experiences and the questions that remain when ordinary explanations have been explored.

01

PARANORMAL INVESTIGATIONS

Investigating reportedly haunted locations using structured methods, observation and documented evidence.

02

PRIVATE EXPERIENCES

Exploring personal experiences and unusual occurrences with an open mind and a measured approach.

03

DOCUMENTATION

Recording investigations through photography, video, audio and written case notes.

OUR APPROACH

EVIDENCE BEFORE ASSUMPTION.

A paranormal investigation should begin with questions, not conclusions.

01

OBSERVE

Understand the location, environment, people, conditions and potential sources of contamination.

02

RECORD

Capture relevant activity using appropriate photographic, video and audio equipment.

03

QUESTION

Challenge unusual observations and consider ordinary explanations before reaching conclusions.

04

REVIEW

Examine the evidence afterwards rather than relying solely on what was experienced in the moment.

EVIDENCE

WHAT WE LOOK FOR.

Evidence should be documented, reviewed and presented with enough context for people to make their own assessment.

PHOTOGRAPHY

VISUAL EVIDENCE

Photographs are examined for environmental, technical and natural explanations before unusual interpretations are considered.

AUDIO

RECORDED SOUND

Audio recordings are reviewed carefully with attention to background noise, location conditions and possible contamination.

VIDEO

RECORDED ACTIVITY

Video provides context that can be reviewed after an investigation and compared with observations made at the time.

EXPERIENCE

WITNESS ACCOUNTS

Personal experiences matter, but they are treated as observations to investigate rather than automatic proof of a paranormal event.
