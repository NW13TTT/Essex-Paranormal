MEET LEWIS

Biography - Lewis McDiarmid

OWNER & LEAD INVESTIGATOR, ESSEX PARANORMAL

My name is Lewis McDiarmid, and I am the owner and lead investigator of Essex Paranormal.

I have always had an interest in the paranormal and the unexplained. Over time that interest developed into a commitment to investigate reported activity properly, document what happens and remain honest about what the evidence does and does not show.

Essex Paranormal is built around honesty, truth and accountability. I will never deliberately mislead people about an investigation or manufacture evidence for entertainment.

Essex Paranormal was originally created by my mother, who had a unique team of people who explored and investigated the phenomena. She was heavily invested in truth and clarity, and she built Essex Paranormal on that ethos. The group eventually disbanded in the early 2000s after many team changes and a name change.

Last year, after much thought and consideration, I decided to reboot Essex Paranormal and return it to its original ethos and principles.

What makes Essex Paranormal different is that it is totally evidence-based and reality-based. No trickery, no staged events, no faked activity. I look at every angle of truth and fact to ensure honesty and accountability are always upheld.

Investigations & Evidence

Essex Paranormal uses a minimum amount of equipment because I believe the most powerful tool is myself.

I do use cameras, night-vision systems, field cameras and similar equipment to gain footage after dark. I also use trigger objects as a precursor to gaining interaction between realms.

Ouija boards, crystal balls, sensory equipment, and historical information relating to the environment are also part of my process. Essex Paranormal also uses drones to gather information and aerial data of locations when required.

My Experiences & Beliefs

My personal experiences are too many to mention, but throughout the site I will share as many as I can so people can read them and hopefully gain some insight into them all.

I believe there is definitely something other than what we consciously choose to be aware of. I believe in what I see, feel, hear, and understand.

Visitors to Essex Paranormal should understand that everything I do is based on truth, fact, and evidence-based material that I always endeavour to collect honestly. Nothing I say, do, share, or post will ever be fake, trickery, or false. Essex Paranormal is proud to be honest and truthful.

What Paranormal Investigation Means to Me

A paranormal investigation is an important part of my life and my journey to try and understand what else is out there. I love being able to explore and understand what the real answer is to life and the afterlife. If there really is a continuation from one stage of awareness to the next, then I will continue to pursue the search for it.
