SUMMARY OF ACTIVITY

LONG PERIODS OF QUIET.

Overall activity was subtle but notable, with long periods of quiet punctuated by clear, unexplained events. The most significant occurrences were disembodied voices and unaccounted movement in empty areas of the tower.

DOCUMENTED PHENOMENA

WHAT WAS RECORDED.

  1. Unexplained Movement

    Description: Multiple instances of shifting/stepping sounds heard from rooms with no visible occupants.

    Location: Interior rooms on upper levels.

    Notes: No team members present in the areas at the time. Sounds were distinct enough to be perceived as movement rather than building noise.

  2. Disembodied Voices

    Description: Voice-like sounds captured during quiet periods of the investigation.

    Characteristics: Short, conversational tones. Not linked to any investigator's position or radio use.

  3. Two Male Voices in Empty Room

    Description: Two clearly audible male voices heard having a conversation in one of the rooms.

    Verification: Room confirmed empty at the time of the event. Heard live by the team and flagged immediately. Marked for detailed audio review in post-analysis.

    Significance: Considered the strongest anomaly of the night due to clarity, duration and location control.

KEY DOCUMENTED EVENT

Two clearly audible male voices were heard having a conversation in an empty room. The room was confirmed empty at the time, the event was heard live by the team and it was flagged immediately for detailed audio review in post-analysis.

PRELIMINARY CONCLUSION

FURTHER STUDY.

Grain Battery Tower did not present constant high-level activity; however, the clear vocal event involving two male voices in an empty room, combined with repeated instances of unexplained movement, makes this location operationally significant for further study.

The Site Is Recommended For Follow-Up Investigations Focusing On:

  • Extended audio monitoring in the room where the conversation was heard.
  • Controlled isolation experiments with reduced team presence.
  • Comparative sessions at different tidal and weather conditions.
