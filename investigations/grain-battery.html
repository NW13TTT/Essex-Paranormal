01 · The Location

Documenting the Site.

The photographs document Grain Battery as encountered by Essex Paranormal, including the coastal setting, defensive structure, exposed concretework, access areas and surrounding foreshore.

The photographic record includes wide establishing views alongside closer photographs of the structure. This provides visual context for the location rather than presenting isolated details without their surroundings.

The images are intended to preserve what was physically present during the visit and provide a visual record of the environment in which the investigation took place.

Location Record

These photographs are presented primarily as documentation of the location and investigation. An image is not automatically treated as paranormal evidence simply because it was captured during an investigation.

03 · The Investigation

On Location.

The field photographs show members of the Essex Paranormal team moving through the location, examining access points and carrying investigation equipment.

The record contains both documentary and posed photographs. Their purpose is to show the people, equipment and physical environment involved in the visit.

The photographic material can also provide useful visual context when reviewing the wider investigation, particularly where the physical surroundings may be relevant to observations or reported experiences.

04 · The Environment

Foreshore & Surroundings.

The coastal setting forms an important part of the visual record. Tide, open water, weather, changing light and the exposed structure all form part of the physical environment documented during the visit.

Wide photographs and the aerial view provide additional context around the structure rather than treating the building as an isolated location.

06 · Investigation Record

Honest. Real. Unbiased.

This page is a photographic record of Grain Battery and the Essex Paranormal investigation environment. The images document the location and the people present without automatically assigning a paranormal explanation to anything visible within them.

Where specific photographs are later reviewed as part of an evidence assessment, those findings can be documented separately with the relevant context, methodology and explanation.

Essex Paranormal's approach is to distinguish documentation from interpretation and to remain honest about what an image can and cannot establish.
