THE LOCATION

HMP
ASHWELL

Behind the quiet Leicestershire fields stands HMP Ashwell… a prison long abandoned, yet never truly empty.

Its cell blocks sit in silence, but the silence feels wrong — heavy, watching.

Visitors report cold pockets that move on their own… distant footsteps echoing through corridors stripped bare… and murmured voices drifting from cells that haven’t held a prisoner in years.

Some say the walls remember every fear, every outburst, every final moment that unfolded behind the bars.

And when darkness settles over Ashwell… the past begins to stir.
