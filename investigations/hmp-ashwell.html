CASE FILE 01

THE LOCATION.

HMP Ashwell was a former prison located near Oakham in Rutland. The site became the subject of considerable interest following its closure and the changes that followed.

For Essex Paranormal, the location represents an opportunity to examine a place with its own history and atmosphere without beginning with a predetermined paranormal conclusion.

The purpose of an investigation is not simply to find something unusual. It is to document what happens, consider what may have caused it and distinguish between an unexplained observation and evidence of something genuinely significant.

A Historical Setting

Former institutional buildings can contain layers of history, environmental changes and human experiences. These factors are important when considering reports or observations made at a site.

Essex Paranormal therefore approaches the location as both a historical environment and an investigation site.

INVESTIGATION METHOD

OBSERVE. DOCUMENT. REVIEW.

Essex Paranormal's approach is based on observation and documentation rather than assuming that every unusual event has a paranormal explanation.

Environmental conditions, natural causes, equipment behaviour and human activity all need to be considered before an observation can be described as unexplained.

01.

Observation

Record what is actually observed without adding a conclusion before the evidence has been reviewed.

02.

Documentation

Preserve observations, environmental information and relevant investigation material for later review.

03.

Review

Question unusual observations and look for ordinary explanations before considering a paranormal possibility.

EVIDENCE STANDARD

An unexplained event is not automatically paranormal. Essex Paranormal's objective is to document honestly, investigate carefully and remain willing to accept whatever explanation the evidence supports.

INVESTIGATION NOTES

WHAT WE LOOK FOR.

During an investigation, attention is given to changes in the environment, unusual sounds, movement, visual observations and other experiences that may require further examination.

Equipment can assist with documenting these events, but equipment readings or unusual recordings are not treated as proof simply because they appear unusual.

Context Matters

The history and physical characteristics of a location can provide important context. Buildings can produce unexpected sounds, temperature changes, reflections, shadows and electromagnetic effects for entirely ordinary reasons.

Understanding those possibilities is part of responsible paranormal investigation.
