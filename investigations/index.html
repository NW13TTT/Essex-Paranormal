EP-001 · PUBLISHED CASE FILE

HMP ASHWELL.

Behind the quiet Leicestershire fields stands HMP Ashwell… a prison long abandoned, yet never truly empty. Its cell blocks sit in silence, but the silence feels wrong — heavy, watching. Visitors report cold pockets that move on their own… distant footsteps echoing through corridors stripped bare… and murmured voices drifting from cells that haven’t held a prisoner in years. Some say the walls remember every fear, every outburst, every final moment that unfolded behind the bars. And when darkness settles over Ashwell… the past begins to stir.

CASE FILE · EP-001

The Site HMP Ashwell.

HMP Ashwell was a former prison site in Rutland. The investigation forms part of Essex Paranormal's evidence-led approach to documenting locations associated with reported paranormal experiences.

Essex Paranormal approaches an investigation without assuming that unusual experiences have a paranormal cause. The location, environmental conditions, observations and recorded material are considered as part of the investigation and subsequent review.

The purpose is to document what happens, examine what can be explained and preserve anything that may warrant further examination.

Evidence Before Assumption

Reported experiences are treated as reports. They are documented and reviewed rather than automatically presented as proof of paranormal activity.

Visitors have also described hearing murmured voices or voice-like sounds apparently originating from areas of the former cell blocks.

INVESTIGATION METHOD

Evidence Led.

01

Observation

The location is observed carefully during the investigation. Reported experiences and environmental conditions are considered without assuming a paranormal explanation.

02

Documentation

Cameras, audio recording and other investigation equipment can be used to document events and provide material that can be examined after the investigation.

03

Review

Recorded material and observations are reviewed after the investigation so that possible explanations can be considered away from the immediate atmosphere of the location.

ESSEX PARANORMAL

Investigation Principles.

Honest

Investigation material should be presented honestly. An unexplained event is not automatically labelled paranormal simply because an immediate explanation is unavailable.

Real

Investigations focus on genuine locations, genuine experiences and what was actually observed or recorded during the investigation.

Unbiased

Evidence is considered on its own merits. Environmental, natural and ordinary explanations remain part of the review process.

INVESTIGATION STANDARD

Evidence Review

Material gathered during an investigation should be considered in context. Environmental conditions, ordinary explanations and the limitations of recording equipment are considered before an event is described as unexplained.

EP-001 · CASE CONCLUSION

HMP Ashwell

HMP Ashwell remains a location associated with numerous reported experiences and a powerful atmosphere created by its history and former use. Essex Paranormal's approach is to preserve the distinction between what is reported, what is observed and what can genuinely be supported by the material gathered. The investigation is therefore presented as a documented case file rather than a claim that every unexplained experience has a paranormal cause.
