EVIDENCE STANDARD

UNEXPLAINED DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY MEAN PARANORMAL.

Essex Paranormal approaches every case without a predetermined conclusion. Environmental conditions, equipment behaviour, human activity, historical context and other conventional explanations are considered before a paranormal interpretation is made.

Where evidence remains inconclusive, it is reported as inconclusive. The archive exists to document what was experienced and recorded, not to manufacture an answer.
