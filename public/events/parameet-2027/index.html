ESSEX PARANORMAL IS COMING TO PARAMEET 2027!

A Midsummer Night’s Scream

Essex Paranormal is delighted to announce that we’ll be exhibiting at ParaMeet 2027, one of the UK’s major paranormal conventions!

Taking place at the stunning Sketchley Grange Hotel in Hinckley, Leicestershire, ParaMeet brings together paranormal investigators, enthusiasts, researchers and some of the most recognisable personalities in the paranormal community.

And this year, Essex Paranormal will be right there amongst them!

COME AND MEET ESSEX PARANORMAL

Founder and lead investigator Lewis McDiarmid, known as The UK’s Most Terrified Investigator, will be hosting the Essex Paranormal stand, alongside fellow investigator and collaborator Jake from Investigating with Jake.

Whether you’re a seasoned paranormal investigator, a curious newcomer or simply fascinated by the unexplained, we’d love you to come along, introduce yourself and discover what Essex Paranormal is all about.

WHAT CAN YOU EXPECT AT OUR STAND?

We’ll have a selection of exclusive Essex Paranormal merchandise available, alongside information about our investigations, upcoming events and opportunities to join us on future paranormal adventures.

We’ll also be showcasing The Terrified Investigator, Lewis McDiarmid’s paranormal book series.

Visitors will have the opportunity to browse and purchase physical copies of the books, meet the author and discover the real-life experiences that inspired the stories.

Prefer to read digitally? We’ll also have information on where to purchase the books through Amazon, including Kindle editions.

And don’t forget to pick up some of our free Essex Paranormal promotional material!

EXPERIENCE THE PARANORMAL

ParaMeet 2027 promises a packed programme of guest speakers, paranormal investigations, fascinating discussions, live podcasts and much more.

It’s an opportunity to meet like-minded people, exchange experiences and celebrate everything that makes the paranormal community so extraordinary.

We look forward to meeting you there!
