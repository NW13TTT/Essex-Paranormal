👻 ESSEX PARANORMAL — THIS SATURDAY! 👻

BRAMHAM BIGGIN — PROJECT BIGGIN

Saturday 28 November 2026

This Saturday, I’ll be heading to Bramham Biggin for what promises to be a fascinating night of paranormal investigation.

Access to this mysterious historic building is extremely limited, but through an exclusive arrangement between Haunted Happenings and the Bramham Estate, I’ll have the opportunity to step inside and explore a location where there are no established haunting narratives or familiar ghost stories to influence what we might experience.

And that, for me, makes it all the more intriguing.

Throughout the night, we’ll be taking part in a series of structured investigations, combining traditional investigative techniques with modern paranormal equipment.

From EVP sessions and sensory deprivation experiments to thermal imaging, the Estes Method, the Ganzfeld Experiment and solitary vigils in complete darkness, the aim is to observe, document and question anything unusual that might occur.

No preconceived ideas. No expectations. Just an opportunity to investigate somewhere genuinely unfamiliar.

Will we experience anything unexplained? Will the building reveal something of its past? Or will the night pass without incident?

There’s only one way to find out.

I’ll be approaching this investigation with an open mind, a healthy dose of scepticism and, knowing me, probably a fair amount of apprehension!

After all, there’s a reason they call me…

THE UK’S MOST TERRIFIED INVESTIGATOR! 👻

Stay tuned for updates from Bramham Biggin.
